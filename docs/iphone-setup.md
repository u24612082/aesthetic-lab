# iPhone gym app: offline logging, home sync

The iPhone keeps a downloaded copy of the tracker and food library. Log workouts, technique scores and meals at the gym without reaching the Mac. When home, open the app on the same Wi-Fi with the Mac awake. Pending changes sync automatically on opening, foregrounding, reconnecting and approximately every 20 seconds while open. There are no recurring exports or prompts.

## Connect once

1. On the Mac, open **Setup iPhone Access.command** in this workspace. It creates a private HTTPS listener in addition to your existing desktop address. Open **Settings → Connect your iPhone** in the tracker.
2. Click **Show profile on Mac**. In Finder, right-click `iphone.mobileconfig` → Share → AirDrop → your iPhone. Only this public certificate profile is transferred. Never share files ending in `-key.pem`.
3. On iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → install **Aesthetic Lab — My Mac**. Then Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → enable full trust for **Aesthetic Lab Personal Mac CA**. These are normal iOS approval screens you must complete yourself. [Apple explains the trust setting](https://support.apple.com/102390).
4. Scan the QR shown on the Mac, open in **Safari**, then Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Open the installed Home Screen app before pairing, so its own storage and cookies are initialized.
5. On the Mac, generate a pairing code in the same guide; enter those six digits in the Home Screen app. The code works once for five minutes. The long `.env` token remains an alternative for initial desktop login; it never appears in the QR.
6. In the installed iPhone app, open **Settings → Connect your iPhone → Prepare for offline use**. Wait for **Ready to log lifts and body weight offline**. This confirms that the app screens, including Stats, and your routines have downloaded and been saved on the phone. Food and pictures download separately; you can log weights without waiting for them. The app also prepares automatically after pairing.
7. Before leaving home, turn on Airplane Mode and reopen the Home Screen app. Check that your workout opens, then turn Airplane Mode off. If preparation reports a certificate error, complete the full-trust setting in step 3 first; proceeding past a browser certificate warning alone does not prepare offline startup.

For this Mac the friendly address is shown in the guide, such as `https://Yarivs-MacBook-Air.local:8443/`. Keep using the same address and installed app; a different hostname/origin has separate browser storage.

## Daily use

- At the gym: use the same Home Screen app normally. Log lifts and reps, mark sets complete and finish workouts offline. In **Stats**, tap **Log body weight** and type your weigh-in. **Saving on this iPhone…** changes to the saved status once the phone confirms its IndexedDB write. Workouts and weigh-ins recover when the browser is closed and reopened. All exercises, effort/technique fields, and the rest timer remain available. Food changes enter a separate durable queue; common-food search, gram weights, portions, favorites, custom pack labels, diary edits/repeats/deletions and nutrition targets work from the downloaded library.
- At home: wake the Mac, connect to the same Wi-Fi and open the app. **Saved to Mac** and a cleared food queue confirm delivery. **Sync to Mac** offers an immediate retry. You do not need Tailscale for this workflow.
- To change an exercise today: tap the full-width **Replace exercise** button under its name, search the local exercise library, confirm the replacement’s sets and load, then tap **Swap for this workout**. Checked sets remain under the original exercise, with a **Replaced for this workout** status, and the replacement has its own Replace button and history. If the replacement is already in the session, its extra sets are added to the existing card. The saved routine stays unchanged for next time. Swaps also work offline.
- Matching exercises share their last working weight across all routines, including the original plan’s separate per-day exercise IDs. A later 36 kg session becomes the next default even if an earlier day used 38 kg; the PR stays separate. Incline/flat and different equipment remain separate exercises. Original sets and routine templates are preserved.
- Each exercise shows **All-time max** and **Most reps** directly on the card. Each record pairs the load with the reps actually performed at that load. Tap **History & previous working sets** for a rep-best table at each weight and dated working-set history. The complete session at the heaviest load stays available during deload weeks. Records also appear in the exercise’s info screen and work offline. Only checked working sets count; higher load-only entries are labelled when reps are unavailable, and unlogged exercises show an explicit empty state. Lighter sessions preserve earlier records.
- The active timer stays at the top while scrolling. Its controls stay clear of the bottom navigation.
- If both devices added different workouts, they combine automatically. If both edited the same sets/entry, the phone preserves its pending copy and shows a conflict. Food delivery uses immutable change IDs with atomic SQLite receipts, so retrying after a lost acknowledgment does not duplicate entries. Local food nutrient snapshots are preserved if the catalogue changes before sync.
- Keep pending work on the same installed app until it syncs. Do not clear that app’s website data or uninstall it while unsynced. A conflict offers a downloadable phone backup and an explicit choice to keep the Mac copy; recovery backups also stay on the device.
- If pairing expires, your downloaded log remains accessible offline. Use **Pair to sync** at home to reconnect; a new pairing is not required for each workout.

New Claude reviews and Open Food Facts searches/barcode lookups need the Mac connection. Previously downloaded coaching/analytics are snapshots and can be stale. The food cache contains the full local catalogue and the most recent 90 days of diary entries. SQLite stays exclusively on the Mac; the phone never opens or copies its live database.

**iOS sync behavior:** delivery happens while the app is running in the foreground. Returning home with the app closed is not a guarantee of background delivery. Open it once at home to let queued changes finish. Physical iPhone certificate installation, Safari and Home Screen behavior still require an on-device check; browser automation covers offline/reconnect behavior at the iPhone 16 Pro’s 402 × 874 logical viewport and landscape.

## If startup does not finish

The app displays **Opening your saved gym log…** immediately, before its main code loads. A missing app file or a stalled phone-storage request displays **Reload app**, keeping saved entries intact. Close other tabs for this app and retry; reconnect to the Mac at home if the offline download needs repair. Do not clear website data to troubleshoot a pending workout. App updates retain the previous build’s cached files so a suspended workout can resume while the new version installs.

If a new button is missing, the Home Screen app may still be running a cached older version. On home Wi-Fi with the Mac awake, use **Settings → Update app**. An older worker can finish downloading the new app but remain waiting, leaving **Updating app…** on screen until its timeout. Allow the download to finish, then fully close and reopen the Home Screen app once; keep its storage and installation intact. The repaired worker no longer holds navigation events open during update checks. Future installed updates also show an **Update app** banner, including when a suspended page missed the original update event. Updating confirms pending local saves and keeps offline logs without requiring another pairing.

The old HealthKit bridge is documented separately in [iphone-healthkit-legacy.md](iphone-healthkit-legacy.md); it is not used by this tracker.
