# Manual nutrition and exercise-specific rest

## Food catalogue and diary

The database contains 7,793 entries with complete energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat from the official USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy CSV archive, plus the original 11 South African starter items, two NutriTech Open Food Facts label references and any user-added products. This is a local reference library, not an API subscription or a food-photo estimator. Original branded seeds remain explicitly illustrative until pack verification. USDA entries show their source and named preparation; they are generic references, not verified South African labels.

After preparing the phone, search runs against its downloaded food library without a Mac connection. Before preparation, the Mac offers a bounded search. Relevance ranks plain staples before unrelated baby food/restaurant meals. Multiple search words, spelling aliases (including spagetti and nutritec), categories, recent foods, favorites and South African synonyms are supported. Raw/cooked names and weight basis are preserved. Available source portions carry explicit gram weights; two large raw eggs convert to 100 g edible portion and 143 kcal in this dataset. Grams remain editable and authoritative. Natural size/preparation variation remains; weigh food when precision matters.

Nutrition, Coach and Analytics render directly in the React app and share its theme and page scroll. Food logging uses a top-layer dialog outside the route container; on phones it fills the available screen and adapts to the visual viewport. Opening, focusing fields, changing food source, saving or closing a dialog keeps the background diary in place.

The diary has an energy progress ring, three macro counters, user-entered optional targets, four meal groups, manual search/custom labels, edit/repeat/remove, recent foods and favorites. Manual targets remain available and pause the adaptive planner when saved. The optional phase-based calculator is documented in [adaptive nutrition](adaptive-nutrition.md). An empty diary means zero recorded intake, not proof of fasting. Editing a diary quantity scales the original immutable nutrient snapshot; a later catalogue correction does not silently rewrite older entries. New repeated entries use the current food label.

Import provenance, archive SHA-256, nutrient IDs and release are in `aesthetic_lab/data/usda_provenance.json`. Reproduce with:

```sh
curl --fail --location 'https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_csv_2018-04.zip' -o /tmp/usda-sr.zip
.venv/bin/python scripts/import_usda.py /tmp/usda-sr.zip
```

The importer includes only finite, complete per-100-g macro records within supported ranges and retains available gram portions. Seeds are inserted by stable keys without overwriting user label corrections, favorites or diary history. Data is included in the source archive and the installed Python package; startup needs no food-data network request. One Food, brand or barcode field searches the downloaded catalogue immediately; submitting with the search icon or keyboard performs a cached, rate-limited global Open Food Facts lookup with cc=za context and South African results first. There is no separate country tab. External full-text search runs only on explicit submission, per OFF rate-limit guidance, never per keystroke. Missing macro fields are excluded; known kJ energy is converted to kcal. Selecting an online barcode saves it in the shared catalogue and phone storage. Upstream failure preserves local results. Brand data remains community-supplied and should match the exact pack.

Source: [USDA FoodData Central downloads](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/). USDA reference data is public domain; attribution/provenance are retained. Open Food Facts has its own attribution/license terms.

## Rest allocation

Checking a set starts an exercise-labelled rest timer automatically. Suggested starting values are 90 seconds for isolation/accessory work, 150 seconds for upper-body compound movements and 180 seconds for lower-body compounds. A logged RIR <=1 or RPE >=9 adds 30 seconds; low-rep compound sets (<=6 reps) add another 30 seconds. Unknown effort is not treated as failure. Suggested rests are capped at five minutes.

These values are transparent implementation heuristics, not measured recovery or individually validated optima. Longer rests for demanding work are informed by [this resistance-training rest-interval trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26605807/); that study does not establish the app's exact exercise-by-exercise durations.

A fixed setting beside each exercise overrides automatic adjustments and is saved in the shared tracker state. The live timer can be extended/shortened in 15-second steps or skipped. Its deadline is saved in the active session, so refreshing resumes the remaining time. It does not send one database write per countdown tick. Supersets rest after matched rounds; completing the entire workout stops the timer. Completing one exercise can still start rest before the next exercise, while all exercise cards stay visible.

The optional settings page explains that rest is now configured per exercise. No camera, AI call or paid service is used to allocate rest. The timer runs in the open web app and catches up from its deadline when the tab resumes; browser suspension and notification behavior still depend on the device/browser.


## Foods from the bulk and cut PDFs

**Nutrition → Log food** opens the **Plan foods** collection: 32 ingredients and preparation variants from both meal plans, including Coco Pops, Jungle Oats, your Clover Authentikos plain Greek-style yoghurt, Pick n Pay low-fat long-life milk and NutriTech Premium Mass vanilla reference, eggs, fruit, rice cakes, hazelnut spread, gainer powder, chicken, rice, spaghetti, vegetables, sauces, cheese, oils, honey, syrup, drinks and pure creatine. Typing searches the whole downloaded catalogue; All, Recent and Favorites remain in that same search interface. Membership never changes your favorites.

Quick gram buttons reproduce explicit food weights from the PDFs, such as 50/70 g cereal, 80 g dry oats, 100/250 g yoghurt, 80/150/250/300 g cooked rice and 120/150 g dry spaghetti. Nothing is added to the diary until **Add to diary** is pressed. Dry and cooked foods remain separate; the app does not assume a fixed cooking yield. Existing database egg/cake/tsp portions are available, and gram amounts remain editable. Milk volume and an unspecified protein scoop are not silently converted to invented gram weights.

Existing USDA rows supply generic reference nutrition; new packaged entries use published sources: [South African Coco Pops reference](https://www.fatsecret.co.za/calories-nutrition/kelloggs/coco-pops/100g), [Tiger Brands Jungle Oats foodservice reference](https://www.tigerbrandsfoodservicesolutions.com/product-categories/jungle/Jungle-Oats-10-kg), and [pure unflavoured creatine reference](https://genesisnutriforce.co.za/creatine-monohydrate/), accessed 2026-11-02. Additional brand references use [Clover's plain Authentikos label](https://www.clover.co.za/product/yoghurt/authentikos/double-cream-plain-yoghurt-750g/), [Pick n Pay's 1%-fat milk table](https://www.pnp.co.za/pnp-live-well-low-fat-long-life-milk-6-x-1l/p/000000000000349244_CS), and [NutriTech's general Premium Mass label](https://www.nutritechfit.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/NutriTechfit-Premium-Mass-Builder-15kg-typical-nutritional-information.png). The NutriTech panel is a 2016 base-formula reference; the user's current Vanilla Soft Serve pack is not independently verified. No scoop weight is assumed. Clover's published protein is 5.5 g per 100 g, not the generic strained-yoghurt figure. The PnP table explicitly uses per 100 g; volume-to-weight conversion is not assumed. Generic dairy and protein alternatives stay in All rather than the default personal collection. Their values are references until matched to the user's actual pack. The oat reference converts its listed 1,584 kJ per 100 g to kcal. Milk, yoghurt, powders, syrup, oils and sauces vary by product. Published PDF meal totals are not used as per-food nutrition labels, and no PDF calorie goal replaces the adaptive plan or current targets.

The curated bindings and optional gram weights live in `aesthetic_lab/data/diet_plan_foods.json`. Startup adds missing products by stable seed keys, preserving label corrections, favorites, logged nutrient snapshots and targets. Plan metadata and portions travel in the full offline food snapshot; the new catalogue version forces preparation on previously paired phones. Ordinary diary sync also refreshes the current plan-food metadata. Open food search also refreshes when a catalogue download completes. Once prepared at home, this collection and its gram previews work offline.
